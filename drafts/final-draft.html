About BRIDGES 2027

BRIDGES 2027 will continue the mission of making software engineering more accessible, inclusive, and globally connected. The summer school will focus on practical software solutions, responsible AI, open-source collaboration, and emerging technologies in the context of Papua New Guinea and the wider Pacific region.
