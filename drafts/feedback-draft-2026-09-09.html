About BRIDGES 2027

The Summer School is designed to provide participants with practical software solutions in a unique region of the world, while advancing the goal of making software engineering universal—accessible to anyone, anywhere, without technical, cultural, or educational barriers.

Core Focus Areas

Research

Gain early feedback on research ideas and seek potential collaborators.

Education

Learn about current trends in software engineering and emerging technologies.

Promotion of Young Researchers

Support young researchers in the early stages of their careers.

Venue

Proposed Venue: Loloata Island Resort

📍 Port Moresby, Central Province, Papua New Guinea

BRIDGES 2027 will take place at Loloata Island Resort, located on an island off the coast of Port Moresby, the capital of Papua New Guinea. Known as the Land of the Unexpected, Papua New Guinea is home to more than 850 languages and has a diverse culture, flora and fauna.

Venue confirmation and further information on transportation, accommodation, and travel arrangements will be announced soon.
